About The Palm Unity

The Palm Unity is a Zen Living address by The Palm Group in Kudasan, Gandhinagar — Kudasan’s only 20-storey residential tower, set on a rare dual-road frontage. It offers spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK flats and penthouses with 18-feet cantilevered balconies, gathering foyers on every floor, four Sky Bridges, a Terrace Garden and The Sky Hall high above the city.

About The Palm Group

Founded in Gandhinagar in 2017 by civil engineers Dinesh Santoki and Jashu Patel, with over three decades in construction behind them, The Palm Group designs and builds every project under one roof. From The Palm Altezza in Sargasan to The Palm Unity in Kudasan, each home is shaped by our own architects and engineers, from the first sketch to handover.
