What we collect

When you verify on this page we collect your name, mobile number and (if you add it) your profession. While you browse, we note which sections you view and for how long, which floor plans or brochure you open, questions you ask the assistant, any visit or callback request, and whether you share the page — if someone opens the link you shared and verifies, we note that they came through you.

Why

To confirm your number, to give you access to project details, and so our sales team can follow up on WhatsApp or phone with information relevant to what you looked at.

Who sees it

Only The Palm Group's sales team. We do not sell your data. Questions you type into the assistant are processed by our AI service provider to generate answers.

Advertising measurement

This page uses the Google tag so we can tell whether our Google ads bring visitors who go on to verify. When you verify, your mobile number is converted into an unreadable code (hashed) in your browser before it is shared with Google, only to match the verification with an ad click. If you arrived from a Google ad, we also keep the ad's click ID with your enquiry.

On your device

We store a sign-in token, the referring salesperson's code and, if you came from a Google ad, the ad's click ID in your browser so you don't need a new code each visit. Clearing site data removes them.

Your choices

To stop contact or ask us to delete your details, write to the email on our website or reply STOP on WhatsApp.
